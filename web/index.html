How to use

  1. Pick Inputs or Outputs, then a port. Use + Port to add another molecule.
  2. Pick an atom and click a grid cell. Erase removes an atom and its bonds.
  3. Choose Bond, choose a bond type, then click two atoms. Clicking the same pair again changes its type.
  4. Enable production to add chambers, conduits and vials with the form rows.
  5. Build and load, then press Solve. Text/JSON editors remain under the collapsed section.
